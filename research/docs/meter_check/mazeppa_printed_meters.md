Source: scores/real/pdf/user-local-aef694ee.pdf (16 pages, rendered at 150 and 300 dpi; every system looked at, doubtful spots cropped and upscaled).

Bar counting: the scan prints bar numbers at the start of most systems (4, 7, 9, 11, 13, ... 198). I used those numbers and checked the barlines between them. The first bar of music is bar 1.
